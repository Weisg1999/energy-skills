This file records energy-skills releases in reverse chronological order. Current version **v1.9**, data cutoff **2026-11-12**. Repository entry point: [README_EN.md](README_EN.md); Chinese version: [CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md).

### 2026-11-14 (v1.9 maintenance: background themes expanded to white, cream, light green and dark)

- **Four themes**: the former light/dark pair becomes `light` (pure white), `cream`, `green` (light green) and `dark`. `knowledge/assets/tokens.css` gains a full set of `--paper / --paper-2 / --card / --ink / --line / --control-line` tokens plus eleven category tints for cream and green, while the dark tokens stay unchanged; a new `--wash` token drives the second radial gradient behind reading pages. Cream and green both declare a light `color-scheme`, and print falls back to pure white.
- **Entry and popover**: the moon/sun binary button on reading pages, the hub and the instance overview becomes a palette button that opens a four-option popover with colour swatches, a check on the active theme and `aria-pressed`. Topbar order remains language → theme → more (contents joins below 900px), and the fixed hub control keeps its corner position. The popover styles live in the new shared `knowledge/assets/theme-menu.css`, loaded by the hub, reading pages and the instance overview.
- **Persistence**: choices are stored in `localStorage['es-theme']` and shared across the hub, all 101 knowledge-module pages, 18 skill pages and the instance overview. A first visit without a manual choice follows `prefers-color-scheme`; later system changes never override a manual choice. Each page's inline head script accepts all four values, so reloads do not flash the default palette.
- **Interaction and accessibility**: opening focuses the active option; `ArrowUp/Down`, `Home/End` move, `Esc` closes and returns focus to the button, and an outside click or `Tab` closes the menu. Options expose `aria-pressed`, and the button's `aria-label`/`title` follow the page language ("选择主题" / "Choose theme"). The popover stays fully inside a 320px viewport.
- **Verification and scope**: all four themes and the open popover were checked at 1440, 390 and 320px with no horizontal overflow, together with click switching, persistence after reload, persistence across pages and keyboard operation. `build --check`, consistency and `git diff --check` pass. This is a UI-only change; body text, module/instance counts and the search index are unchanged.

AVC tracks availability, adjustment qualification, reactive-call qualification and bus-voltage qualification, commonly 98%/96%/90% or 99%/99%; Anhui uses 0.03% per percentage point with a 0.5% per-item cap. Primary frequency follows GB/T 40595-2021 (dead zones by source type, roughly ±0.02-0.067 Hz; droop 2%-6%, recommended 5%; lag ≤1 s) and GB/T 30370-2013 (thermal ±0.033 Hz, droop 3%-6%, 75% in 15 s).
- **Penalties and exemptions**: a 20-row penalty table (trigger, calibre, cap, grade) and six exemption categories (grid cause, force majeure, dispatch instruction, planned maintenance/test, unit state, policy pilot), plus seven non-exempt cases. Exemptions must be filed within the publication window with evidence; late or unsupported claims are treated as the plant's responsibility.
- **Falsifiable example (grade D)**: a synthetic 300 MW coal unit with 150,000 MWh monthly on-grid energy and a CNY 0.40/kWh assessment price incurs 2,437.5 MWh / CNY 975,000, or 1.63% of monthly revenue; primary frequency, dispatch discipline and forced outage together account for 92.3%, while per-point AGC is only 0.3%. Under the stricter southern 0.1%/0.1 pp version, primary frequency would cost CNY 6 million without the cap; the 0.5% cap cuts it to CNY 300,000 — a 20× difference. The qualified rate must rise to 99.9% to save CNY 240,000 a month; 90%→99% remains capped and saves nothing.
- **Indexes and updates**: wired into both READMEs, `knowledge/INDEX_EN-2026-11.md` and `knowledge/索引与口径-2026-11.md`; records Shandong Doc. 52 (effective 2026-10-01), Central China Doc. 129, the southern-region 2024-12-18 revision, the 2026 North/East China drafts and the unverified Shanxi document number.
