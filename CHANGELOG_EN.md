### 2026-11-05 (v1.6 addendum: Grid-connection assessment and the "two detailed rules" knowledge module)
- **New knowledge note**: [grid-connection assessment and the two detailed rules 2026-11](knowledge/并网运行考核与两个细则-2026-11.md). It answers "how does the two-rules assessment work, how are AGC/AVC/primary-frequency calculated, what can be exempted, and how much does it cost"; it is a knowledge module, not a standalone skill. Ancillary-service markets and settlement stay with Electricity Trading Expert, grid standards and tests with Renewable Project Development, and assessment deductions with Storage Investment & Operations.
- **Document system**: the national layer is Doc. [2021] No. 60 (grid-connection operation management) and No. 61 (ancillary services), both dated 2021-12-21 and covering 35 kV and above; regional/provincial rules set the parameters; project documents (dispatch agreement, test reports, monthly settlement statements) set the boundary. The NEA published a **draft** revision on 2026-09-30 that would rename "assessment" to "management" and add force-majeure and first-of-a-kind relief — a draft, not current law.
- **Formulas and thresholds**: AGC combines K1/K2/K3 into Kp (principle cap 2; component floors and caps commonly 0.1 and 2), with 60 s for thermal and 20 s for hydro; the southern region assesses daily on-grid energy × (1 − qualified rate) × 0.1%, and Shandong's 2026 storage rules use performance benchmarks K1/K2/K3 = 1.3/1.7/1.4. Primary frequency follows GB/T 40595-2021 (dead zones by source type, roughly ±0.02-0.067 Hz; droop 2%-6%, recommended 5%; lag ≤1 s) and GB/T 30370-2013 (thermal ±0.033 Hz, droop 3%-6%, 75% in 15 s).
- **Penalties and exemptions**: a 20-row penalty table (trigger, calibre, cap, grade) and six exemption categories (grid cause, force majeure, dispatch instruction, planned maintenance/test, unit state, policy pilot), plus seven non-exempt cases. Exemptions must be filed within the publication window with evidence; late or unsupported claims are treated as the plant's responsibility.
- **Falsifiable example (grade D)**: a synthetic 300 MW coal unit with 150,000 MWh monthly on-grid energy and a CNY 0.40/kWh assessment price incurs 2,437.5 MWh / CNY 975,000, or 1.63% of monthly revenue; primary frequency, dispatch discipline and forced outage together account for 92.3%, while per-point AGC is only 0.3%. Under the stricter southern 0.1%/0.1 pp version, primary frequency would cost CNY 6 million without the cap; the 0.5% cap cuts it to CNY 300,000 — a 20× difference. The qualified rate must rise to 99.9% to save CNY 240,000 a month; 90%→99% remains capped and saves nothing.
